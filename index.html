Why Sharely?

Splitting a home with roommates usually means scattered chats, guesswork about who paid what, and rent decisions made on gut feeling. Sharely puts your income, expenses, savings goals and shared bills in one place so decisions are based on real numbers, not memory.

It runs entirely in your browser — no server account to wait on, and your financial details never leave your device.

How it works

  1. Create your account. Takes under a minute — everything is saved locally in this browser.
  2. Set up your plan. Enter your income, other costs and a savings goal to get a recommended rent range.
  3. Track as you go. Log expenses, split bills with roommates and set a goal for something you're saving for.
  4. Check in. Your dashboard and the AI Advisor surface trends and answer quick questions about your numbers.
